How to write it

    
Plain Markdown. Put a line with three dashes between slides, or leave them out and each # or ## heading starts a new one. The layout comes from what is on the slide. The full format has every rule.

    

      
---
title: My talk
subtitle: One line on it
author: Your name
theme: paper
length: 5
---
Optional settings at the very top. Length is in minutes, for your timer.

      
# My talk
The first slide, if it is just a big heading, is the title card. Later, a slide with only a heading opens a section. Start it with a number, like # 1. Why, for a big section number.

      
### The problem
## People stall on step three
A small heading above the main one becomes the label over it.

      
Notes: What you say here.
<!-- or in a comment -->
Your script, shown only in your window. Write (click) where the next thing should appear.

      
- **68%** finish setup
- **1.1** days to a first project
A list where every line starts with a number becomes big stats.

      
> The quote itself.
> — Who said it
A quote on its own becomes a pull quote, with the dash line as its credit.

      
![A view of the office](office.jpg)
A picture alone fills the slide. With other things it sits on the right.

      
### Keep
One line.
### Change
One line.
Two to four small headings, each with something under it, become cards.

      
- **Team**: Three people
- **Length**: Eight weeks
Label and value lines line up in two columns.

      
---
accent: #FF5A36
background: #111116
text: #FFFFFF
font: Space Grotesk
body-font: Inter
---
Your own colors and fonts, on top of any design. Fonts come from Google Fonts. Leave out any you don't need.

      
One sentence, nothing else.
Becomes a big statement. Tables and code blocks work too, and slides with lots of words get smaller type on their own.

Help make Stagehand better

    
Stagehand is open source under the MIT license. Everything that makes it work is on GitHub, and it improves fastest when the people giving talks with it change it. You don't need to be a developer to help.
