How to write it

Plain Markdown. Put a line with three dashes between slides, or leave them out and each # or ## heading starts a new one. The layout comes from what is on the slide.

---
title: My talk
subtitle: One line on it
author: Your name
theme: paper
length: 5
---

Optional settings at the very top. Length is in minutes, for your timer.

# My talk

The first slide, if it is just a big heading, is the title card. Later, a slide with only a heading opens a section. Start it with a number, like # 1. Why, for a big section number.

### The problem
## People stall on step three
