The problem

New customers were stalling on step three

They connected an account, then stopped, and most never came back.

When we started, the pattern was clear in the numbers. People signed up, connected an account, and then stopped on step three. (pause) Most of them never came back. So we went and asked them why.

I connected my account and then I didn't know what I was supposed to do next.

Customer interview, number four

We talked to eleven people. This line, from the fourth interview, came up in some form in almost all of them. They had done what we asked. Nothing told them what came next.

How we worked

Small team, short loops

TeamTwo developers, one designer LengthEight weeks RhythmInterview Monday, ship Friday
A word on how. It was three of us, for eight weeks. Every Monday we talked to a customer. Every Friday we shipped whatever that conversation changed. Short loops kept us honest.
